Mission Explorer

Seven space missions, replayed step by step in 3D with the photographs they sent home. Pick one to follow its route.

BepiColombo, Artemis II, Voyager 1, Voyager 2 and Mars Express trajectories are from NASA JPL Horizons. Apollo 11 and Apollo 13 are gravity-model reconstructions, not flight telemetry; Beagle 2’s final descent is modelled from Mars Express’s pre-release path. BepiColombo’s route after 20 November 2026 is a Horizons prediction, not a completed flight. Photographs are from NASA, ESA and Wikimedia Commons; each carries its credit and licence. Planet surfaces in the 3D view are generated illustrations, not photographs.
