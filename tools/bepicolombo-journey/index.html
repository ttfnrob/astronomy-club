BepiColombo: the long road to Mercury

A step-by-step 3D journey from the mission’s origins to Mercury orbit, made for Watling Astronomy Club.

Trajectories for BepiColombo, Artemis II, Voyager 1, Voyager 2 and Mars Express come from NASA JPL Horizons. Apollo 11 and Apollo 13 are reconstructed with a gravity model from the published burn times and altitudes, so they are close approximations, not flight records. Beagle 2’s final descent is calculated by coasting Mars Express’s pre-release path. Photographs are from NASA and ESA (ESA images CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO) and have been resized; each carries its credit. Planet surfaces in the 3D view are generated, not photographs.
